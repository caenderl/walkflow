Your treadmill, in your own hands

Walkflow connects to your treadmill over Bluetooth and controls it from your computer: workouts with steps, real routes on a map, and your history. Free, open source, no account.

Download Open in the browser

For treadmills with Bluetooth FTMS. Tested with the Sportstech sPad 500 so far. More on compatibility

How it works

No setup beyond Bluetooth. You can try everything with the built-in demo treadmill before you connect a real one.

  1. ConnectSwitch the treadmill on and connect over Bluetooth. Or start the demo, which needs no treadmill.
  2. Pick what to doTrain freely, choose a workout, or walk a route and see where you are on the map.
  3. StartWalkflow brings the belt up to speed in small steps. Stop is always one click, or the space bar, away.

What it can do

The treadmill’s own display is limited. Walkflow gives it a proper screen.

Routes with a map

Walk a real place. Import a GPX file from your route planner, or take one from the route store. The map shows where you are along the route, with progress and the time you have left.

  • Routes from the project’s store, with waypoints and updates
  • Open maps (OpenStreetMap), switched on only if you want them
  • Your own routes can be exported and shared as files

Workouts with steps

Build a workout from steps of time or distance, each with its own speed. A week plan keeps your weekly minutes in view and suggests what to do today.

  • Steps by time or by distance
  • Speed changes in small steps, never in jumps
  • Import and export workouts as files

Your history

Every run is saved on your device. See your distance per day for the last 30 days, your streak and your best week, and export runs as CSV or TCX.

  • Saved automatically, on your device only
  • Backup and restore as one file
  • Export for other fitness apps

Private by design

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to give away.

No account, no cloud

Your runs, workouts and routes stay on your own device.

No tracking

No analytics, no cookies, no ads, no scripts from third parties.

Open source

MIT licence. You can read every line, and you can change it.

Works offline

Install it from the browser or as a desktop app. The map and the route store need the internet; the rest does not.

Please read this first

  • Use it at your own risk. Walkflow is a private open-source project without any warranty.
  • If the Bluetooth connection drops, the app cannot stop the belt. Then only the stop button on the treadmill or its safety key will help. Keep both within reach.
  • If your treadmill has a safety key, use it. Start the belt only while you stand on the side rails.
  • Speed, distance, calories and heart rate come from the treadmill or are estimates, and they can be wrong.
  • Stop if you feel unwell. If you are unsure whether treadmill training suits you, ask a doctor first.

The installers are not signed

Your system will warn you when you open the app for the first time. That is expected, and the file is the one from this project’s GitHub page. You can check it against SHA256SUMS.txt on the same page.

  • macOS: drag the app to Applications and open it. At the warning, choose “Done”, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”.
  • Windows: at “Windows protected your PC”, click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  • Linux: make the file executable (chmod +x) and start it.

Which treadmills work?

Walkflow speaks FTMS (Fitness Machine Service), the standard Bluetooth protocol for treadmills. The manual or the app of your treadmill often says whether it supports it. It has been tested with one treadmill so far, the Sportstech sPad 500; others may behave differently. Results are collected in the test notes. If you try another model, a note in the issue tracker helps everyone.

The web version needs Web Bluetooth: Chrome or Edge on a computer or an Android phone. Safari and iPhone do not support it; use the desktop app instead. The app is available in English, German, French and Ukrainian.
