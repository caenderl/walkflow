Technical facts about the app (as a basis for filling this in, not legal text; remove or adopt before publishing):
  • No accounts, no own server, no cookies, no tracking, no analytics.
  • No scripts, fonts or other content from third parties; everything is loaded from the same address.
  • Bluetooth data from the treadmill is processed only in the browser and sent nowhere.
  • The browser’s localStorage holds only settings: speed limit, maximum change per command, background behaviour, sport for TCX, language, theme, accepted safety notice.
  • A service worker stores the app files in the browser cache for offline use.
  • Recordings (history) stay only in memory until they are exported as CSV/TCX or the page is closed.
  • The desktop app (not this website) asks api.github.com for a newer version on startup; can be turned off.
